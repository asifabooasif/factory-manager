# Factory Agent (Sentinel)

One agent that replaces the separate Guardian chip and AI button with a single living orb. It combines the **Accounting Guardian**, the **AI Assistant** and a new **daily-rhythm intelligence**.

## The orb
* Breathes slowly when all is well. Swirls fast with a wave pill ("Reading your ledger…") while it analyses. Glows green when clear, amber when something needs you, red-pulsing when critical. A red badge shows how many things need you.
* Click it for the panel. **Drag it anywhere** - it snaps to the nearest edge and remembers its place. Double-click to send it home.
* The Guardian chip and the AI button can still be shown (Controls tab). They can be dragged and remembered too.
* Notices grow out of the orb with a soft chime, and are also added to the Notification Center. Animations follow your device's reduce-motion setting.

## What it watches (and tells you what to do)
| It notices | Example |
|---|---|
| **Expenses missing** | Receipts and attendance recorded for 3 working days but no expense vouchers - compared with how often *you* normally record expenses |
| **Wages missing** | Piece wages unpaid over a week; attendance with no wage run after it; last month's salaries not processed |
| **Attendance missing** | Work recorded on days nobody's attendance was marked |
| **Opening data missing** | Items with no opening stock (blocks issues and deliveries; makes profit unreliable), no opening cash or bank balance |
| **System not updated** | No entries for 2+ working days |
| **Receipts not matched** | Money received but not applied to an invoice |
| **Possible double entry** | Same amount, party and date entered twice |
| **Regular expense missing** | Rent or electricity recorded in 3 of the last 4 months but not this month |
| **Everything the Guardian finds** | Control totals, price jumps, sales below cost, BOM over-use, and more |

Each finding says **what**, **why it matters (with your numbers)** and **numbered steps**, with a button that opens the exact screen.

## Telling it what is normal
Press *"No expenses on those days"*, *"Nobody worked"*, *"Already handled"* and it remembers (for all devices) and stops asking about those days. *Snooze 1 day* hides a notice until tomorrow.

## Panel tabs
* **Do now** - the top instruction, today's checklist (money in, expenses, attendance, production) and every open finding.
* **Insights** - start-up progress (items, parties, opening balances, bank, opening stock, employees), a 14-day grid of what was and wasn't recorded, and what it has learned about your rhythm.
* **Ask** - plain questions answered from your data: *what should I do next, what is missing, today, cash balance, who owes us, profit this month, stock of leather, open expenses, report on stock ageing*.
* **Controls** - show/hide each icon, pop-ups, sound, animations, automatic checks, end-of-day time, check interval, reset positions.

## Opening-stock warning
Until every item has opening stock (or is marked as having none), stock, sales-margin, production and profit reports in the Report Suite show a banner that the figures are not final.

## What it will not do
It never posts accounting entries, pays anyone or changes records by itself. It watches, explains and opens the right screen - you stay in control.
